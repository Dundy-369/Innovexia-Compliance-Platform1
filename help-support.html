Frequently asked questions

How long does verification usually take?

Most statutory checks (GST, PAN, Udyam) return within minutes. EPFO and ESIC can take up to 24 hours depending on portal load.

What happens if a bidder's documents don't match portal records?

The bidder is flagged for manual review with the specific discrepancy noted. You can request clarification or disqualify directly from the Compliance verification tab.

Can I edit a tender after it's published?

Yes, before the bid submission deadline. Changes after submissions open trigger a notification to all bidders and may extend the deadline.

How is the compliance score calculated?

It weights statutory compliance, document authenticity, financial stability, and past performance. The exact breakdown is shown on each bidder's Final Assessment tab.

Can bidders see other bidders' compliance scores?

No. Bidders only ever see their own score and status — never another bidder's.
